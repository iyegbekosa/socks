Import from a spreadsheet

Upload a CSV or Excel file. Good rows are saved and any problem rows are listed with the reason, so you can fix them and upload just those rows again.

Start from a template: students template (.csv) · students template (.xlsx) · staff template (.csv) · staff template (.xlsx)

Required: first_name, last_name, sex (M or F), date_of_birth (DD/MM/YYYY), class (for example JSS 1A), guardian_name, and guardian_phone or guardian_email. Imported students get no password: once the import finishes, use the Print passwords buttons on the import's page (one per class) to print their one-time passwords.

Staff files: full_name, then email or phone, and optional roles (comma-separated, default teacher). Each person is sent an invite to set their own password.
